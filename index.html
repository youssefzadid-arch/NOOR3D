Step 1: Browse Trimble 3D Warehouse

Step 2: Import into Scene (.GLB / .GLTF / .OBJ / .STL)

Select any downloaded 3D file from your iPhone. PocketMass automatically normalizes its scale, adds crisp architectural edges, and drops it at ground level.
